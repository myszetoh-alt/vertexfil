VERTEXFIL offers four filament series: PLA Basic, PLA Matte, PLA Silk, and PETG. Each series is designed to suit different creative and functional needs.
The PLA Basic series is available in nine everyday colours: Black, White, Grey, Blue, Red, Brown, Green, Orange, and Yellow.
The PLA Matte series features two colours: Matte Blue and Matte Black.
The PLA Silk series is available in Gold and Silver.
The PETG series is available in three classic colours: Black, White, and Grey.
All VERTEXFIL filaments across every series are produced in 1.75mm diameter, the most widely used size for desktop 3D printers.
It comes down to the look and feel you want for your creation. PLA Basic offers our broadest colour range for everyday projects. PLA Matte provides a low-sheen finish that gives models a modern, non-reflective appearance. PLA Silk delivers a reflective finish in Gold and Silver for designs where appearance takes center stage.
Choose PETG when your project calls for PETG material properties or when you prefer printing with PETG. If your design doesn’t specifically need PETG, any of our PLA series offer a great starting point with more colour and finish options.
