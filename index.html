What filament series does VERTEXFIL offer?

PLA Basic, PLA Matte, PLA Silk and PETG. Each series offers different appearance or material characteristics.

What colours are available in PLA Basic?

Black, White, Grey, Red, Blue, Brown, Green, Orange and Yellow — nine colours in total.

What colours are available in PLA Matte?

Matte Blue and Matte Black.

What colours are available in PLA Silk?

Gold and Silver.

What colours are available in PETG?

Black, White and Grey.

What filament diameter do you sell?

The filament range showcased here uses a nominal 1.75 mm diameter. Always check your printer's supported filament diameter.

Which material should beginners start with?

PLA Basic is generally a convenient starting point for everyday FDM printing. Check your printer's requirements and the intended use of your print.

Where can I check prices and order?

Visit our VERTEXFIL Shopee Malaysia store for current prices and availability. Select the correct filament series and colour on Shopee before ordering.

Can you help me choose between PLA and PETG?

Yes. Tell us your printer model and what you want to print using our WhatsApp contact link. Material suitability also depends on your design and printer settings.
